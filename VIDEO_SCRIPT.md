# Demo video script (about 100 seconds)

**Setup before recording**
1. Terminal 1: `PRESENCE_TIMEOUT_MIN=120 PUBLIC_URL=http://<your-laptop-ip>:3000 npm start`
2. Terminal 2: `npm run seed -- --url http://<your-laptop-ip>:3000` and copy the three printed links.
3. Open the **organiser dashboard** and the **entrance screen** in two browser tabs on the laptop.
4. Open the **check-in link** on your phone (or in Chrome's phone view: F12 → device toolbar → iPhone 12 Pro).
5. Record the screen with OBS, Loom or the Windows Game Bar (Win + G). Record your voice at the same time.

| Time | On screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Entrance screen with the QR code and live headcount | "At a mixer you walk up to strangers with no idea who they are, and organisers can't tell who's even here. RoomRadar fixes both." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Phone: scan / open link, fill the one-minute profile, tap **Check in** | "You scan the QR at the entrance and tell it what you're into, what you're looking for, and what you can help with. Even what you're wearing, so people can find you." |
| 0:25–0:25 | Entrance screen headcount ticks up by one | "The room updates live." |
| 0:25–0:45 | **People to meet** tab: scroll the first two badges, point at the yellow highlights | "Straight away it tells me who to meet, and why. Nisha is looking for tech help, which I offer. Kabir invests, which I'm looking for. It favours two-way matches and rare shared interests, and every card has an opener so the first line isn't awkward." |
| 0:45–0:55 | Tap **Find them** on a card | "Find them shows their badge colour, what they're wearing and where they are in the room." |
| 0:55–1:10 | Tap **Wave** on Kabir. Then on the laptop, in a second browser window checked in as Kabir... *(or simply show the Waves tab where a seeded person has already waved and tap **Wave back**)* | "A wave says 'I'd like to talk'. When both people wave, you're connected and only then see each other's LinkedIn. Contacts are never shown without a mutual wave." |
| 1:10–1:20 | **Who's here** tab: type "fintech" in search, tap the **Terrace** area filter | "Who's here is the live list, searchable by interest and filterable by where people are standing." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Laptop: organiser dashboard, send an announcement, show it appear on the phone; point at "have left" and the CSV button | "Organisers see the headcount, who's left, what the room is into and what people are looking for, can message everyone at once, and export attendance. If someone's phone goes quiet for 15 minutes, they're checked out automatically." |
| 1:35–1:45 | My badge → **Show my badge full screen** | "And when you're trying to find each other, hold up your phone. That's RoomRadar: know who's in the room, and who to talk to first." |

**Tips:** keep it under 2 minutes; zoom the browser to 110–125% so text is readable; don't mention the recruitment or the organisation you're applying to.
